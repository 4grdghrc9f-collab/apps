Deep: Freediving Trainer

Dry breath-hold training and a freediving log for iPhone.

Safety first. Deep is not a freediving course and does not replace an instructor. Breath-hold training in Deep is dry only: sitting or lying down, on land. Never train breath-holds in water alone, never hyperventilate, and always freedive with a trained buddy.

What Deep does

  • Guided breathe-up and recovery breaths.
  • Dry static holds with your personal best and first-contraction time.
  • CO₂ and O₂ tables built from your best, with safety limits.
  • A dive log for pool and sea sessions, with progress towards your next course's minimums.

Your data

Everything you record stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. See the privacy policy.

Independence

Deep is independent. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, AIDA, PADI, SSI, Molchanovs or any other freediving organisation. Course figures in the app come from public course outlines and may change: always check your school's current standards with your instructor.
