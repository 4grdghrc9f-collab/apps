Privacy policy

Deep: Freediving Trainer · last updated 14 November 2026

In short: Deep collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile choices (school, level, goal), training sessions, breath-hold times and dive log are saved in a database on your iPhone only. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Health information

Breath-hold times and dives are personal training records. They never leave your iPhone in this version, and Deep does not read or write Apple Health.

Children

Deep collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Future versions

If a later version adds optional features that send data (for example an account, backup or an AI tutor), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and those features will explain what they send.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: xzy.apps@gmail.com.
